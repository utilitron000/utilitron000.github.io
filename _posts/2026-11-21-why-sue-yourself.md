On 11/12/2026 a [lawsuit](https://www.platkinllp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Irreplaceable-et-al.-v.-Anthropic-et-al.-FINAL-AS-FILED.pdf) was filed in the Superior Court of the State of California by 4 nonprofits alleging that Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta, and SpaceX were violating California's Unfair Competition law. The goal of the lawsuit? To pause the development of more advanced LLMs, and to prevent Recursive Self Improvement (RSI). 
The 63 page complaint lists the plaintiffs as Irreplaceable Education Fund; Public Citizen, Inc.; Heat Initiative, Inc.; and Future Tech Insights Inc., d/b/a The Alliance for Secure AI. On closer inspection, some of these plaintiffs are not like the others. Following the funding of these groups leads us on a wild journey into the heart of Effective Altruism, to the doorsteps of Dustin Moskovitz and Jaan Tallinn, two of Silicon Valley's most notorious billionaire cultists. Roll up your windows and lock the doors, kids... This rabbit hole is in a bad neighborhood!

### AI Harm vs. AI Risk
Although AI Harm and AI Risk sound similar or even interchangeable, the term *AI Risk* has become so entrenched in TESCREAL circles that it now effectively functions as a dog whistle for AI Doomsday cults. When TESCREAL talks about *AI Risk* they are referring to the 'existential risk' posed by an Artificial Super Intelligence that, to use their terminology, is not *aligned* with human values and goals.
By contrast, 'AI Harm' is most often used to describe dangers and harms caused by LLMs and experienced by people today. The focus of AI Harm research is not on some nebulous future risk, but instead on the children who are using LLMs as suicide coaches, nonconsensual deepfakes, algorithmic wage discrimination and pricing, grand theft culture, discriminatory hiring practices, mass surveillance applications, wanton destruction of the environment, and other very real harms currently being done by LLMs.

### The Three Card Monte
The three card monte is one of the most popular confidence games throughout the world, with numerous local variations in every culture across the globe. The game is an evolution of the age-old 'cups and balls' trick that has been performed by illusionists, magicians, sorcerers, and sleight of hand artists across the world since antiquity. Set up in back alleys, often on an unfolded cardboard box (to provide a playing surface that is easy to fold and move), a dealer manipulates 3 cards, usually 2 black face cards and a red Queen. "Follow the red lady," the dealer chants, shuffling and rearranging the cards with a practiced hand. In most games the dealer holds the cash in one of his hands to obscure the sleight of hand being performed. If this doesn't already seem like an apt metaphor for the game being played by the plaintiffs of this lawsuit, one must only explore the monte further. Another hallmark of the three card monte is the group of shills keeping watch for police and surrounding the dealer. In variations the shills are either winning easily or losing, but the goal is always the same: to rope a mark and convince them to play.
